I got my first camera as a child, and photography has been part of my life ever since. For a long time I mostly shot while travelling or at family gatherings, lugged heavy camera bags around, and for that very reason sometimes left them at home. Over the last few years though the hobby has moved more and more to the centre, and I regularly try out new things. Right now I'm most drawn to portrait photography. Every person is unique, and the human interaction makes each shoot something special. But I also just love being out with the camera and keeping an eye out for interesting subjects.

I'm always looking for new faces for my portraits, regardless of age or gender. If you enjoy being in front of the camera and are interested in a collaboration, feel free to get in touch here.

For general enquiries, just send me a message at contact@fcwphoto.com.
