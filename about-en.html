I'm Florin, a photographer based in Basel. I do something completely different for my day job, but photography has become one of my favourite pastimes. That's why I spend a lot of my evenings and weekends out looking for interesting subjects on long photo walks through the city, or, more and more lately, doing portrait sessions in my home studio.

If you're interested in a portrait shoot, I'd love to hear from you here. For general enquiries, just send me a message at contact@fcwphoto.com.
